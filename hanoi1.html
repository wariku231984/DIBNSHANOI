🎮 How to Play

1. Move all disks from Tower A to Tower C.
2. Only one disk can be moved at a time.
3. A larger disk cannot be placed on a smaller disk.
4. Try to complete the game using the minimum number of moves.

Minimum Moves = 2ⁿ − 1
